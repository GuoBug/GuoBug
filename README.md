### 👨‍💻 About Me

Product Architect & Builder focusing on **AI workflow orchestration, enterprise system productization, developer tools, and distributed infrastructure**.

With over a decade of hands-on experience orchestrating product engineering across global developer tooling (GitLab) and 100M+ user consumer platforms (Bilibili, Ctrip), I approach the AI-native paradigm through **AI Pair Programming** and **"Learning by Doing"**. Rather than treating AI as a black-box generator, I treat it as an architectural sparring partner: driving system decisions from real business friction, while collaboratively reasoning through core trade-offs, state slicing, and extreme boundary testing.

A career tested by the rigorous demands of enterprise commercialization and massive consumer-scale traffic:

  * Drove mobile growth initiatives across distribution networks, App acquisition, SEO/SEM, API partner platforms, and checkout conversion funnel optimizations.
